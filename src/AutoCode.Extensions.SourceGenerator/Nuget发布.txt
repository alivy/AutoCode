
打包发布命令：

IF NOT $(ConfigurationName) == Publish  EXIT /B 0  
DEL $(SolutionDir).nuget\$(ProjectName)*.nupkg 		
dotnet pack $(ProjectDir)$(ProjectName).csproj --no-build -c Publish -o $(SolutionDir).nuget


上传命令,会发布Nuget
nuget push $(SolutionDir).nuget\$(ProjectName)*.nuget YOUR_NUGET_APIKEY_HERE -Source https://api.nuget.org/v3/index.json

Nuget APIKey：YOUR_NUGET_APIKEY_HERE
